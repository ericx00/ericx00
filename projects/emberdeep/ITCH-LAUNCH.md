## Marketing on itch.io (in order)

1. **Publish the game and the free starter on the same day.** Link each page to the other two.
2. **Devlog on day 1** on the game page: "A deckbuilder with Heat instead of energy: how the rule works". Devlogs appear in followers' feeds and on itch's devlog browse page.
3. **Devlog on the starter page**: "Free card game assets + the game I built with them". Asset pages with a playable demo stand out.
4. **Release announcements board** on the itch.io community forum: one post for the game. **Game Assets** section: one post for the free starter. One post each; do not repeat.
5. **Jams** (the biggest free traffic on itch): post the free starter in the community tab of running jams where assets are allowed, and submit Emberdeep only to jams whose rules allow existing games. Read each jam's rules first.
6. **Answer every comment within a day** and ship the fix they asked for; then post a short devlog ("v1.1: fixes from your feedback"). Updated projects resurface in "Recently updated".
7. **Collections**: ask players to add it to a collection; make your own public collection "Made with Forge assets".
8. **After 10+ ratings**, put the kit in an itch **sale** (for example 20% off for a week) so it appears on the sales page, and bundle it with your three other packs.
9. Reuse the Reddit and Discord posts from `WEEK1-SALES-SPRINT.md`, now leading with the playable game link. r/deckbuildinggames, r/WebGames and r/roguelites accept free browser games (check each rule page first).

Track: views, plays, ratings, starter downloads, kit sales, donations. Everything is on the itch.io analytics page.
